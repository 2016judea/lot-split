How the check works

The city's Chapter 540 built-form rules set the smallest new lot on Urban Neighborhood ground at __MIN_AREA__ sq ft and __MIN_WIDTH__ ft wide (Table 540-15). A lot can be split in two when it has twice both: __NEED_AREA__ sq ft and __NEED_WIDTH__ ft along the street. On big-lot blocks in Interior 1 and 2 the minimum rises to the average lot within 350 ft (footnote 1); __BIGLOT__ of the passing lots sit on such blocks and were checked against that higher line. Lot area is the county assessor's. Width is read off the county parcel polygon, so a plat may differ by a foot or two; a corner lot is measured along its longer street.

This is a rule check on public dimensions, not a survey, a plat approval or legal advice. The house's position on the lot is not in any public layer, so a passing lot may still need the house moved or removed. The city's subdivision review (Chapter 598) decides.
