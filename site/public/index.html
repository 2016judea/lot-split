How the check works

The city's Chapter 540 built-form rules set the smallest new lot on Urban Neighborhood ground at 5,000 sq ft and 40 ft wide (Table 540-15). A lot can be split in two when it has twice both: 10,000 sq ft and 80 ft along the street. On big-lot blocks in Interior 1 and 2 the minimum rises to the average lot within 350 ft (footnote 1); 57 of the passing lots sit on such blocks and were checked against that higher line. Lot area is the county assessor's. Width is read off the county parcel polygon, so a plat may differ by a foot or two; a corner lot is measured along its longer street.

This is a rule check on public dimensions, not a survey, a plat approval or legal advice. The house's position on the lot is not in any public layer, so a passing lot may still need the house moved or removed. The city's subdivision review (Chapter 598) decides.
